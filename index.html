Questions before you order

Can I walk in without an order?
Yes. Every mini flavor is in the case each day we're open, with a few 4-inch cheesecakes to grab and go. Pre-order if you want a certain flavor or have an event.
Are whole cheesecakes kept in the shop?
Only around Thanksgiving and Christmas. The rest of the year a 9-inch cheesecake is made to order.
How long do they keep?
Seven days in the fridge, or up to 2 months in the freezer. They taste best within the first month. Thaw one at room temperature until it is chilled.
How long can they sit out at an event?
Two hours at most. Keep them refrigerated until you set them out. We don't recommend outdoor displays from June through August.
Can I cancel or move an order?
All sales are final. With 48 hours' notice a cancelled order is eligible for store credit, or you can move your pickup to another day.
Is the kitchen allergen-free?
No. Gluten-free and vegan cheesecakes are prepared separately, but the kitchen works with all the standard baking ingredients, so guests with severe allergies should use caution.
